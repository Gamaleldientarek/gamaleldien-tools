# Numeric Scale Generator - Marketing Plan

## Product Overview
- **Tool Name:** Numeric Scale Generator
- **URL:** https://tools.gamaleldien.com/numeric-scale
- **Target Users:** Figma designers, UI/UX professionals, design systems teams
- **Core Value:** Generate linear and logarithmic numeric scales for Figma variables instantly

## Unique Selling Points (USPs)
1. **Instant Generation:** Real-time preview of numeric scales
2. **Multiple Scale Types:** Linear and 7 different logarithmic ratios
3. **Figma Integration:** Direct JSON export compatible with Figma Variables
4. **Professional Ratios:** Golden ratio, perfect fifths, musical intervals
5. **Design System Ready:** Perfect for spacing, sizing, typography scales

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Figma power users, Design system maintainers
- **Secondary:** UI/UX designers, Product designers, Frontend developers
- **Pain Point:** Manually calculating numeric scales for design systems
