Creative System
Prompt Builder

Structure your prompt with 7 sections — works with Midjourney, FLUX, Sora, Claude, GPT, and any other AI model.

Section 01
The Job
What does this system produce? Be specific. e.g. "Generate cinematic image prompts for FLUX"
Section 02
Target Environment
Which model or platform will receive this prompt? e.g. Midjourney, FLUX, Sora, Claude, GPT-4o
Section 03
Inputs I Will Provide
What will you give the AI? e.g. reference images, brand guidelines, color palettes, descriptions
Section 04
Core Operation
What should the AI do with your inputs? Describe its internal logic and decisions
Section 05
Desired Output
Exact format, structure, length, tone. What must always be included or excluded?
Section 06
Hard Rules & Prohibitions
Absolute rules — never violate. One rule per line.
Section 07A
Interpretation Mode
How should the AI read and fill in information from your inputs?
Section 07B
Missing Info Policy
What happens when inputs are incomplete or missing?
