Brand Strategy Tool

Brand Discovery
Brief

A comprehensive questionnaire designed to uncover the essence of your brand. Your answers will shape the strategy, identity, and visual direction of your brand.

1

About You & Your Business

Let's start with the basics — who you are and what you do.

Q1.What is the exact name you would like used in your brand / logo?

Include any specific capitalization, spacing, or spelling preferences.

Q2.Are there any acceptable abbreviations or alternative name versions?

e.g., Arabic, French, or other language variations. Detail all acceptable versions.

Q3.What industry or sector does your brand operate in?
Q4.Tell us your brand story. How did it start and why?

Every brand has an origin story. Share yours — what inspired you to start, and what drives you today?

Q5.What is the core problem your brand solves for its customers?

Think of it as your reason for existing — what gap do you fill in the market?

Q6.What is your brand's mission and vision?

Mission = what you do today and why. Vision = where you want to be in the future.

Q7.Describe your target audience. B2B, B2C, or both?

Include demographics, behaviors, social class, and what matters most to them.

Q8.Is this a new brand or a rebrand of an existing one?

If rebrand — what's working that you want to keep? What's broken that needs to change?

Q9.What are the expected deliverables?

e.g., Logo, Brand Guidelines, Business Cards, Social Media Templates, Stationery, Packaging, Website, Signage, etc.

Q10.What channels does your brand currently use or plan to use?

e.g., Website, Instagram, LinkedIn, Print, Packaging, Retail, Events, etc.

2

Brand Vision & Personality

Let's define the soul of your brand — how it thinks, speaks, and feels.

Q11.What are the main keywords that define your brand?

Think of it as SEO keywords — words that when searched, your brand should appear.

Q12.What is the main theme or direction the brand should convey?

What is the overarching feeling or idea?

Q13.What is your brand tagline (if you have one) and what does it mean to you?
Q14.What makes you different? What is your main Unique Selling Point?

The one thing you want to be known for above everything else.

Q15.Why do your customers trust you? What keeps them coming back?

Think about the trust signals — what makes people choose you over competitors?

Q16.Describe your brand's personality using 5 words.

e.g., Bold, Innovative, Trustworthy, Minimal, Playful.

Q17.What are the 3 words you want people to say about your brand after experiencing it?

When someone interacts with your brand, what should they feel or think?

Q18.How do you want your customers to feel when they interact with your brand?

e.g., Empowered, Safe, Inspired, Excited, Understood.

Q19.If your brand was a person, describe their personality.

How do they dress? How do they speak? What do they care about?

Q20.If your brand had a voice, how would it speak?

Q21.What is the main message you want to communicate to your customers? In 3 keywords.

e.g., Trust, Innovation, Simplicity.

3

Competition & Inspiration

Understanding your competitive landscape and creative inspirations.

Q22.Who are your main competitors — both local and global?

Please provide names and links if possible.

Q23.What do you like about those competitors, and what you don't like? And why?

Be specific — is it their logo, colors, messaging, tone, website, overall vibe?

Q24.Are there any brands (not necessarily competitors) that you admire or are inspired by?

These can be from any industry. What about them inspires you?

4

Visual Direction

Now let's get into the look and feel — colors, style, and aesthetics.

Q25.What feel or style would you like your brand to convey?

e.g., Minimal, Luxurious, Playful, Corporate, Futuristic, Organic, Bold, Elegant, Raw, Tech.

Q26.What logos or brands have you seen that you LIKE visually? And why?

Provide links or images. What exactly — the icon, colors, typography, overall feel?

Q27.What logos or brands have you seen that you DISLIKE? And why?

Helps us avoid the wrong direction early.

Q28.What colors do you like or expect to be used? And why?

Dark theme, bright theme, earthy tones, neon? Any specific colors?

Q29.What colors do you dislike or feel don't represent your brand?
Q30.Would you like your logo to be an icon, typography-based, or a combination of both?

Icon = symbol (Apple, Nike). Type = lettering (Google, Coca-Cola). Combo = both (Adidas, Burger King).

Q31.Do you prefer a clean, flat, illustration-based style — or a more photographic, textured, detailed style?

Clean & Flat → Apple, Airbnb, Stripe. Photographic & Textured → National Geographic, luxury brands. Or a mix?

5

Additional Notes

Anything else that's important to you.

Q32.Is there anything else you'd like to share? References, mood boards, thoughts, or concerns?

Anything we haven't covered that is important to you.
